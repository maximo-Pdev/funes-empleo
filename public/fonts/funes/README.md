# Self-hosted municipal-theme fonts

These are the original Be Vietnam and Sora font binaries referenced by the
Funes municipal site's business theme, extracted on 2026-10-20. They are not
Be Vietnam Pro and are not a redistribution of the Crafto template.

Theme source: https://funes.gob.ar/views/assets/demos/business/business.css

Source font declarations:
- https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Be+Vietnam:wght@100;300;400;500;600;700;800&display=swap
- https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Sora:wght@100;200;300;400;500;600;700;800&display=swap
