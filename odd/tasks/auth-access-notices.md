# Auth lifecycle notices inside Home's access card

Route: inline implementation with one focused visual/browser proof and an independent read-only review at close (retiring four URLs is a consumed-contract change, risk item 3). No data, migration, dependency or deployment changes.

## Specs
- S1: "/account-suspended: borrar la página y que aparezca el mensaje directamente en el recuadro de login en Home, estilizado acordemente". `/account-suspended` must answer 404 with no `Location`, and the suspended state must render inside Home's access card with the Office contact message and no unusable login form.
- S2: "/recovery-invalid: borrar la página y que el formulario y mensaje aparezca directamente en el recuadro de login". `/recovery-invalid` must answer 404, and Home's card must show the invalid-link message plus the recovery (email renewal) form.
- S3: "/session-expired: borrar la página y que el mensaje aparezca directamente en el recuadro de login". `/session-expired` must answer 404, and Home's card must show the expired-session message together with the login form so the visitor can sign in again.
- S4: "/verification-expired: cambiar el estilo de la página y que quede estilizado acorde a Home". Keep the URL and its "Reenviar verificación" form; restyle with Home's municipal surface (principal green field, white glossy bevel card, shared typography and controls).
- S5: "/verification-pending: borrar la página, es innecesaria y podría parecer como un mensaje informativo en el recuadro de registro". `/verification-pending` must answer 404; the pending-verification information stays covered by the registration card's existing informational response.
- S6: Every retired URL returns a real HTTP 404 (no redirect, no alias), and every remaining destination that used to reach those pages resolves to Home with the matching `?acceso=` state.
- S7: Copies stay truthful and non-enumerating: no internal suspension reason, no account existence, no token or Auth error detail in the URL.
